This update for Thermoo Patches mainly removes functionality from the mod (sad), but comes with greater flexibility for mod pack creators. In the future, the Ad Astra patch [will be replaced by Galacticraft 5](https://github.com/TheDeathlyCow/thermoo-patches/issues/15), but this patch will be maintained separately and not released until Galacticraft itself releases its first alpha.

* Removed Ad Astra patch (mod has been discontinued for Fabric 1.21+, to be replaced by Galacticraft 5)
* Updated Origins patch to Origins `1.13.0-alpha.8`.
* Removed Extra Origins and Mob Origins patches
* The Origins 'Patch' now ONLY provides data pack facilities for Origins; it no longer modifies Origins itself. This is left to modpack authors, who presumably know how to integrate Origins better with other mods in their specific pack. As a consequence, there is no longer out-of-the-box integration for Origins and Frostiful/Scorchful.
* Added more powers to Thermoo Patches for cold vulnerability/resistance/immunity.
* The "cold/heat lover" powers were changed into "ignores cold/heat effects". These powers will now disable Frostiful and Scorchful's temperature effects when applied (though be careful, other mod/datapack effects may still be applied).
